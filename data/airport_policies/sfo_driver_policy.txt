San Francisco International Airport (SFO) - Driver Incentives & Cancellations

Driver Cancellation Rules:
- Driver cancellation rate threshold at SFO is strictly set at 15%.
- If cancellation rates exceed 15% during high-demand windows, automated incentive dispatch triggers.

Driver Incentives:
- Low Severity (Completion Rate 75–84%): $5 per completed trip bonus for drivers queueing in the staging lot.
- High Severity (Completion Rate <75%): $10 per completed trip bonus + priority queue positioning.
- Maximum incentive budget per intervention window is $500 without Senior Director authorization.
