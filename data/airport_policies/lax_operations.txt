Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) - Operations & Queue Policy

Queue Rules & Zone Management:
- Drivers must wait in the LAX-it Staging Area located at 6100 W 98th St.
- Queue jump privileges are granted only to electric vehicles (EVs) and wheelchair-accessible vehicles (WAVs).

Pickup & Drop-off Rules:
- All ride-hail pickups must take place exclusively at the LAX-it lot adjacent to Terminal 1.
- Direct curb pickups at central terminal area (CTA) horseshoe are strictly prohibited.
