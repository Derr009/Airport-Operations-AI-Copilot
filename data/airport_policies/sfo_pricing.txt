San Francisco International Airport (SFO) - Pricing & Surge Rules

Surge Multiplier Limits:
- The default surge multiplier for SFO starts at 1.0x.
- Maximum allowable surge multiplier under normal operations is 1.5x.
- Any surge request exceeding 1.3x up to 1.5x requires automated risk classification.
- Dynamic surge exceeding 1.5x is strictly prohibited by airport regulatory policy.

Approval Escalation Requirements:
- Surge adjustments from 1.0x to 1.2x: Automatically approved by algorithm during peak hours.
- Surge adjustments from 1.3x to 1.5x: Requires explicit Human Operations Manager Approval.
