John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) - Operations Policy

Queue Rules & Zone Management:
- Drivers must hold in Cell Phone Lot 1 or 2 to enter the dispatch queue.
- Maximum staging queue capacity is 250 active vehicles.

Pickup & Drop-off Rules:
- Terminal 4 pickups are restricted to Passenger Pick-up Zone A & B.
- Terminal 5 pickups must occur at the orange ground transportation zone.
