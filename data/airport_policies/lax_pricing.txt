Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) - Pricing & Surge Policy

Surge Multiplier Limits:
- Base surge multiplier begins at 1.0x.
- Maximum allowable surge multiplier for LAX is 1.8x during severe terminal congestion.
- Surge multipliers between 1.4x and 1.8x require Human Operations Manager Approval.
- Surge multipliers > 1.8x are blocked by system guardrails.
