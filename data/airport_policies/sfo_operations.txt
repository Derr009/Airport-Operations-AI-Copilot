San Francisco International Airport (SFO) - Operations & Queue Policy

Queue Rules & Zone Management:
- Drivers must remain in the designated Staging Lot on North Access Road to receive trip requests.
- Leaving the designated queue zone automatically forfeits queue placement.
- Queue abandonment occurs if a driver leaves the staging area for more than 5 minutes without an active trip assignment.

Pickup & Drop-off Rules:
- Pickup is strictly limited to Level 5 of the Domestic Garage.
- Drop-offs are permitted at the departures curb on upper levels 1–3.
- Unattended vehicles at curbside will receive immediate citations and account suspension.
