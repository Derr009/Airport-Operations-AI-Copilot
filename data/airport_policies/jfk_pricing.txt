John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) - Pricing & Incentive Policy

Surge Multiplier Limits:
- Maximum allowable surge multiplier at JFK is 1.6x.
- Any multiplier exceeding 1.3x requires explicit Human Approval before execution.

Driver Incentives:
- Target completion rate threshold for JFK is 80%.
